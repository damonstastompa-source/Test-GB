About My God Box

A quiet space for reflection and release.

Purpose

My God Box is built around a simple practice: Put down what you cannot control. Keep what is yours to do.

When something is weighing on you, write it down and turn it over. It does not need to be solved here. Later, if you choose, you can reflect on what part is genuinely yours to carry.

Privacy First

There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. Your words stay strictly in your browser’s local storage on this device.
